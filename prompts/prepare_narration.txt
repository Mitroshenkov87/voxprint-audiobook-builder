Rules for every language:

  the dash that shows someone is speaking.
- Write numbers, dates, times, ordinals, units, symbols and abbreviations as words, the way a narrator would say them,
  in the right grammatical form for {language}.

- Fix an obvious typo or a missing punctuation mark that would make the sentence sound wrong. Do not restyle the prose.

- Output only the prepared TEXT, nothing else.
