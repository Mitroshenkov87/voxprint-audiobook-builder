Voxprint icon assets - third-party icon attribution
====================================================

The fingerprint glyph used in voxprint.svg / voxprint.png / voxprint.ico /
voxprint-setup.ico / alt1.png / alt2.png is derived from the "fingerprint"
icon of Tabler Icons (outline set, version 1.6).

  Source : https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons
           https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tabler/tabler-icons/main/icons/outline/fingerprint.svg
  License: MIT License
  Copyright (c) 2020-2026 Pawel Kuna
  Retrieved: 2026-10-03

Modifications: the five stroke paths were scaled up, recoloured with a
cyan/teal-to-blue gradient stroke, thickened for small sizes (16/24/32 px
variants use a subset of the paths / heavier stroke), and combined with
original sound-wave bars (not part of Tabler Icons) on a dark rounded tile.
The dark tile, gradient, bars and installer arrow badge are original work.

Splash artwork (splash.jpg)

splash.jpg (wheat field and mountains with the Voxprint icon) is original artwork

attribution above.
