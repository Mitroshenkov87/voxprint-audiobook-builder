Voxprint icon and splash - original artwork
===========================================

The application icon and the start-up splash are original artwork made for
this project by Aleksandr Mitroshenkov. They are not third-party material
and they are not derived from Tabler Icons.

Icon
----
assets/voxprint.png is the square icon: one fused mark, a fingerprint whose
right ridges become sound-wave bars, cyan to blue, on a dark navy rounded
tile. assets/voxprint.ico and assets/voxprint-setup.ico are built from that
PNG and are what the window, the taskbar, the installer and the uninstall
entry use. installer/linux/voxprint-256.png is the same mark at 256 px for
the Linux desktop entry.

Splash
------
assets/splash.jpg is the square splash: the same logo in a painterly alpine
evening (darkening blue sky, golden rye, pale haze, a soft red glow), with
no houses, no river and no text. ui/splash.py loads this filename.

Easter egg
----------
assets/easter-egg.jpg is original generated artwork, no real person's likeness.
ui/splash.py shows it, with the caption easter_egg.caption, after a hidden
click sequence on the splash.

Suite action icons
------------------
assets/icons/ holds the save, save-as, save-copy, export and download drawings.
See assets/icons/LICENSE.txt. They are original drawings (Apache-2.0).
